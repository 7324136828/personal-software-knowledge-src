# Multiple Linear Regression II
# Twenty recall cards on categorical predictors, joint inference, ANOVA, and interactions.
What is the key idea behind binary variables?	A factor with c categories is represented with c-1 binary indicators when an intercept is present; the omitted level is the reference.
What is the key idea behind reference levels?	Changing the omitted category changes coefficient interpretations and individual t-ratios, but not fitted values or the overall model fit.
What is the key idea behind general linear hypothesis?	Joint restrictions are expressed as H0: Cβ=d, with C chosen for the scientific question.
What is the key idea behind partial f test?	Compare a reduced nested model with a full model: F=((Error SS)reduced-(Error SS)full)/(p s²full).
What is the key idea behind term life example?	Testing MAR0 and MAR2 jointly gives F=3.235 with p=2 and 269 denominator degrees of freedom; the reported p-value is 0.0409.
What is the key idea behind one-factor anova?	The model yᵢⱼ=μⱼ+εᵢⱼ estimates each group mean by its sample average and partitions total variation into factor and error sums of squares.
What is the key idea behind auto claims anova?	For 18 risk classes and 6,773 claims, log-claim ANOVA reports Factor SS 39.2, Error SS 7729.0, and Error MS 1.14.
What is the key idea behind ancova?	A factor and covariate can have variable intercepts with a common slope, or both intercepts and slopes can vary by group.
What is the key idea behind hospital costs?	The pooled plot suggests a negative association, but DRG-specific relationships show why an omitted factor can mislead; the variable-slope test is F=13.535.
What is the key idea behind interactions?	Interactions permit effects such as slopes to differ by category; with two factors they can be interpreted as a combined factor.
What is the key idea behind general linear model?	Continuous, transformed, and categorical predictors can enter linearly in coefficients; coefficient estimates can be nonunique under linear dependence.
What is the key idea behind unique fitted values?	Even when parameter estimates are not unique, least-squares fitted values, residuals, and error sums of squares are unique.
Why are only c-1 indicators used for a c-level factor with an intercept?	All c indicators sum to one, creating perfect linear dependence with the intercept.
What does a partial F test compare?	A reduced model that imposes the null restrictions with a full nested model.
What is the least-squares estimate of μⱼ in one-factor ANOVA?	The group sample mean, ȳⱼ.
What does an interaction allow in an ANCOVA model?	The covariate effect, such as a slope, may differ across factor levels.
What remains unique in a linearly dependent general linear model?	Fitted values, residuals, and error sums of squares remain unique even if coefficients do not.
______ is the omitted factor category used as the baseline for interpreting indicator coefficients.	The reference level.
The factor sum of squares equals total SS minus ______.	error SS.
In the term-life example, marital status is assessed by testing ______ jointly.	the MAR0 and MAR2 coefficients.
