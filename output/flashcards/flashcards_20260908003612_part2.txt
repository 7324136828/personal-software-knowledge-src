# Multiple Linear Regression I
# Recall deck covering least squares, model assumptions, goodness of fit, inference, and special explanatory variables from Chapter 3. This is part two of a two-part series.
What is the coefficient of determination \(R^2\)?	\(R^2=\dfrac{\mathrm{Regression}\ SS}{\mathrm{Total}\ SS}\), the proportion of variability explained by the regression function.
What is the relationship between \(R^2\) and the correlation of \(y\) with fitted values?	\(R^2=[r(y,\widehat y)]^2\).
Why can ordinary \(R^2\) be misleading for variable selection?	It never decreases when an explanatory variable is added, even if the added variable is not useful.
What is the adjusted coefficient of determination?	\(R_a^2=1-\dfrac{(\mathrm{Error}\ SS)/[n-(k+1)]}{(\mathrm{Total}\ SS)/(n-1)}=1-\dfrac{s^2}{s_y^2}\).
How are \(s^2\) and \(R_a^2\) related as measures of fit?	Better fit means smaller \(s^2\) and larger \(R_a^2\); minimizing \(s^2\) is equivalent to maximizing \(R_a^2\).
What are the degrees of freedom in the multiple-regression ANOVA table?	Regression: \(k\); Error: \(n-(k+1)\); Total: \(n-1\).
What is the standard error of coefficient \(b_j\)?	\(se(b_j)=s\sqrt{\text{the }(j+1)\text{st diagonal element of }(\mathbf{X}'\mathbf{X})^{-1}}\).
What hypothesis is usually tested to determine whether explanatory variable \(x_j\) is important?	\(H_0:\beta_j=0\) versus \(H_a:\beta_j\ne0\).
What is the t-ratio for a regression coefficient when testing \(H_0:\beta_j=0\)?	\(t(b_j)=b_j/se(b_j)\).
How is \(t(b_j)\) interpreted?	It is the number of estimated standard errors that \(b_j\) is away from zero.
For a two-sided t-test at significance level \(\alpha\), when is \(H_0:\beta_j=0\) rejected?	Reject when \(|t(b_j)|>t_{n-(k+1),\,1-\alpha/2}\).
What rule of thumb is given for a variable's importance in a large sample?	A variable is important if its t-ratio exceeds two in absolute value.
What is the \((1-\alpha)\) confidence interval for \(\beta_j\)?	\(b_j\pm t_{n-(k+1),\,1-\alpha/2}se(b_j)\).
What is an added variable plot?	A plot of residuals from regressing \(y\) on the other explanatory variables against residuals from regressing \(x_j\) on those same other variables.
How is the added variable plot for PRICE versus ECOST constructed in the refrigerator example?	Regress PRICE on RSIZE, FSIZE, SHELVES, and FEATURES to obtain \(e_1\); regress ECOST on those variables to obtain \(e_2\); then plot \(e_1\) versus \(e_2\).
What does a partial correlation coefficient measure?	The correlation between \(y\) and \(x_j\) in the presence of the other explanatory variables.
How can a partial correlation coefficient be calculated from a t-ratio?	\(r(y,x_j\mid\text{others})=\dfrac{t(b_j)}{\sqrt{t(b_j)^2+n-(k+1)}}\).
Why might an added variable plot be preferable to a partial correlation coefficient alone?	The plot can reveal nonlinear relationships that a correlation coefficient may miss.
What are omitted variables, and why can they be problematic?	They are variables left out of a regression; omission can make coefficients strongly significant when they should not be or even give them the incorrect sign.
What is a binary explanatory variable also called?	An indicator variable or dummy variable.
In the model \(LNFACE=\beta_0+\beta_1LNINCOME+\beta_2SINGLE+\varepsilon\), how is \(\beta_2\) interpreted?	It is the expected change in LNFACE when moving from SINGLE \(=0\) to SINGLE \(=1\), holding LNINCOME fixed.
Why can linear regression represent nonlinear functions of explanatory variables?	The regression function must be linear in parameters, but explanatory variables can be transformed nonlinear functions such as \(x^2\), \(\ln x\), or products.
What is polynomial regression as a special case of linear regression?	Use \(x_1=x,x_2=x^2,\ldots,x_p=x^p\) in \(\mathrm{E}\,y=\beta_0+\beta_1x_1+\cdots+\beta_px_p\).
What is an interaction term?	A variable created as a nonlinear function of two or more explanatory variables, commonly \(x_3=x_1x_2\).
In \(\mathrm{E}\,y=\beta_0+\beta_1x_1+\beta_2x_2+\beta_3x_1x_2\), what is \(\partial\mathrm{E}\,y/\partial x_1\)?	\(\beta_1+\beta_3x_2\).
Why cannot \(x_3=x_1+x_2\) be used as an estimable interaction-like explanatory variable alongside \(x_1\) and \(x_2\)?	It is a linear combination of existing explanatory variables, so all parameters cannot be estimated.
What is piecewise linear regression?	A model with linear relationships in regions separated by one or more kinks, often using binary variables to join the pieces.
